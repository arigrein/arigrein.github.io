The Workbench

A log of the hardware I am currently breaking, fixing, and experimenting with.

Lenovo Thinkpad P14s AMD Gen 6

My primary daily driver. Powered by a Ryzen AI Pro 7, I use it for college coursework, mobile app development, and wrestling with locally hosted LLMs.

Dell XPS 8920 Desktop (Pop!_OS)

My main rig (i5, 1050ti) until the summer heat forces me to migrate to a cooler room with a laptop. I swapped in a heavily abused, recycled RX 580 to see what it could do—it lasted a few months before dying, so the stock 1050ti is back in action.

2013 MacBook Air (Pop!_OS)

Formerly my main app dev machine. I accidentally nuked the OS, rebuilt it, and am currently engaged in a bitter war trying to get it to connect to my home network because modern Wi-Fi protocols are too complicated for its old Broadcom chip.

Toshiba 57H93 (2004 Rear-Projection CRT TV)

I spent countless hours trying to force this into serving as a second monitor via DVI. Linux xrandr tools are much better than Windows for fixing overscan, but the TV fought back hard. I finally found the closest-to-perfect setting—and promptly lost it when I broke the OS on my MacBook.

2002 Compaq Presario 1700T

Running Windows XP. A fun time capsule for retro tech and playing Age of Empires II. My next goal is to get Puppy Linux running on it, which requires burning a physical CD because booting from USB didn't even exist yet.
